Apptrix is built on [Fyne](https://fyne.io). The pieces we write that are useful
beyond our own product, we release — so any Go developer can use them, whether or
not they ever open Apptrix.

Everything below lives under the
[ApptrixAI organisation](https://github.com/ApptrixAI) on GitHub. Issues and pull
requests are welcome.
